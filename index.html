Paying

Jobs under $400 are paid upfront. Bigger jobs take a 50% deposit. Monthly packs are billed at the start of each month.

Changes

Two rounds of changes are included. Extra rounds are quoted before we start.

Tools

We use AI tools in production. Art direction, design and final polish are done by hand.

How it works

Most packs are done in under a week.

  1. Send the details

    Your text, date, logo and any photos. A phone photo of an old flyer works.

  2. Pay and get on the calendar

    Pay by card with the button next to your package, or we send you a secure link with your delivery date.

  3. See a first draft

    Within 3 days. Tell us what to change, twice if you need to.

  4. Get your files

    MP4s in three sizes plus a print-ready PDF, ready to post.

About
